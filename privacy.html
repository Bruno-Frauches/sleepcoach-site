Privacy policy

Sleep Coach · effective October 16, 2026 · updated November 8, 2026 (optional anonymous errors and usage)

Sleep Coach works offline. It has no accounts of its own, no ads and no advertising tracking, and the developer receives none of your data unless you agree to send errors and usage data without personal identification (see "Anonymous errors and usage").

What stays on your device

Your creature collection, inventory, recipes, teams, profile and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone.

Screen reading and screenshots

Screenshots you import and screens read in live mode are recognized on the device itself (Google ML Kit, running locally). No image is sent to any server.

Live mode uses Android screen capture and a bubble over the game. It only runs when you turn it on, shows a notification while active, and pauses outside the game.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and saves a copy of your data in the app's hidden folder in your own Google Drive (drive.appdata permission). The app cannot read any other files in your Drive. The developer has no access to this backup, and your email is only used by Google to sign you in.

What the app downloads

To keep game data up to date, the app downloads creature, ingredient and recipe information and images from Neroli's Lab (nerolislab.com) and a public game-data service (pokeapi.co), and event news from the game's official website. These requests carry none of your data. As with any internet request, those sites may see your device's IP address.

Report a problem (optional)

In Settings › Game art › About the app › Report a problem, the app builds a text with the version, the device and the latest errors kept on the device itself. Nothing is sent on its own: you see the text and choose to send it by email to support (sleepcoach.sup@gmail.com) or share the file. The text does not include your collection, your account email or game data.

Log and diagnostics (optional)

In Settings › Help improve the app, you can turn on the log. Off (the default), the app records nothing. On, the app writes to a file, on the device only, what it read and calculated, without your name, email or account data. A screenshot only enters the log if you tap "Mark moment" in the bubble menu; the file keeps up to 50 images and 10 MB. The log never leaves by itself: it only reaches support (sleepcoach.sup@gmail.com) if you tap "Send by e-mail" and send the message. You can delete the log at any time. Testers' diagnostic mode works the same way: the log stays on the device and leaves only if you share it.

Anonymous errors and usage (optional)

The first time you turn on live mode, the app asks whether you want to help improve Sleep Coach. You can accept or decline, and live mode works either way. With no answer, or if you decline, nothing is sent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Anonymous errors and usage.

If you accept, the app sends data with no personal identification to Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Analytics): app crashes (the app area and the event name, without the error text), the app and Android versions, the device model, which game screen was read, whether the reading worked and how long it took, which app tabs were opened, and whether the scan limit or a locked Pro feature was shown. The app never sends screenshots, names, email, your collection, inventory or game data.

This data is tied to an identifier generated for this installation of the app, not to you. The advertising ID and ad personalization are turned off. The developer uses the data only to fix crashes and decide improvements, and does not sell it or use it for advertising. Google processes the data as a service provider under the Firebase privacy policy (firebase.google.com/support/privacy). If you decline or turn it off, the app stops sending; what was already sent follows Firebase's retention period. Because the data does not identify you, the developer cannot link it to a person.

Sleep Coach Pro subscription (optional)

The app is free. When available, the Sleep Coach Pro subscription (monthly or quarterly, with a 14-day free trial) unlocks extra features. Purchase, billing and cancellation are handled by Google Play: the developer does not receive or store payment data, or the name or email of your account. On the device, the app keeps only the trial start, the end of the paid period confirmed by Google Play and a daily scan counter, to know which features to unlock; none of this leaves the device (except in your own Drive backup, if you turn it on). The subscription renews automatically and you can cancel any time in Play Store › Subscriptions. To recover the subscription on another device, use "Restore purchase".

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device. No server sends notifications.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Errors and usage are sent only if the person using it accepts, and the data sent does not identify them.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device.
  • To delete the backup, open Google Drive on the web › Settings › Manage apps › Sleep Coach › Options › Delete hidden app data. To remove the app's access to your account, use myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps & services.
