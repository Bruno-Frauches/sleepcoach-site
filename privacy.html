Privacy policy

Sleep Coach · effective October 16, 2026

Sleep Coach is an offline app. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your device

Your Pokémon collection, inventory, recipes, teams, profile and settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone.

Screen reading and screenshots

Screenshots you import and screens read in live mode are recognized on the device itself (Google ML Kit, running locally). No image is sent to any server.

Live mode uses Android screen capture and a bubble over the game. It only runs when you turn it on, shows a notification while active, and pauses outside Pokémon Sleep.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and saves a copy of your data in the app's hidden folder in your own Google Drive (drive.appdata permission). The app cannot read any other files in your Drive. The developer has no access to this backup, and your email is only used by Google to sign you in.

What the app downloads

To keep game data up to date, the app downloads Pokémon, ingredient and recipe information and images from Neroli's Lab (nerolislab.com) and PokéAPI (pokeapi.co), and event news from the game's official website. These requests carry none of your data. As with any internet request, those sites may see your device's IP address.

Diagnostics

Diagnostic mode writes a log on the device only. It leaves your phone only if you share it yourself.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device. No server sends notifications.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data on the device.
  • To delete the backup, open Google Drive on the web › Settings › Manage apps › Sleep Coach › Options › Delete hidden app data. To remove the app's access to your account, use myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps & services.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Questions: use the developer contact email on the app's Google Play page.
