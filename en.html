Sleep Coach

Recipe, inventory, team and collection planner for Pokémon Sleep players.

  • Ingredient inventory, recipes and the weekly pot, with Sundays and events included.
  • Weekly planner and best-team suggestions for your island.
  • Collection with a rating for each Pokémon and hints on which ones are worth investing in.
  • Reads the game's screens on your phone, from screenshots or the live-mode bubble.
  • Everything stays on your device. Optional backup to your own Google Drive.
